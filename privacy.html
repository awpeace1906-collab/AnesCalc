AnesCalc Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to the AnesCalc iOS and iPadOS app

In short: AnesCalc collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. AnesCalc does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or health information. The app contains no third-party software development kits, analytics or advertising services, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To save you re-typing, the app keeps the following in the device's local app storage. It is never sent anywhere:

  • Calculator inputs such as age, weight, height, sex, laboratory and hemodynamic values, and the export date.
  • Your display preferences (color theme and light/dark mode) and whether you have acknowledged the clinical disclaimer.

The patient identifier (MRN) field is session-only. It is held in memory while the app is open and is never written to storage. Altitude is also never stored. You can clear saved inputs at any time with the app's reset function. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Location

If you tap the location button next to the Altitude field, the app asks for permission and then reads your device's altitude once to fill in that field. Your location is not stored, not tracked and not transmitted. If you decline, the app works normally and you can type the altitude yourself. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

PDF export

When you export a PDF, it is created on your device. It leaves the app only if you choose to share or save it through the iOS share sheet, and it goes only to the destination you pick. We never receive a copy.

Children

AnesCalc is a clinical reference intended for licensed healthcare professionals and clinical trainees. It is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's GitHub issues page.
